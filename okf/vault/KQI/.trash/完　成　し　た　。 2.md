$$\tau_i=f\{S,G,B,Q,C,D,M\}$$
$$S:\text{Stillness},G:\text{Genesis},B:\text{Buffer},C:\text{Coherence},D:\text{Depth},M:\text{Motion}$$

主観目線の定義(束の間の夏休みの前提条件)$$\bar{S}(t)=(1-\alpha_S)\bar{S}(t-1)+\alpha_SS(t)\qquad\gamma(t)=\frac{S(t)}{\bar{S}(t)+\epsilon}\quad(\epsilon>0)$$$$\quad\frac{d\hat{t}}{dt}=\gamma(t)\qquad\hat{t}(t)=\int_0^t\gamma(u)du$$
$$D(\hat{t})=\int_{0}^{\hat{t}}S(u)du\qquad\because\tau_i=\frac{D(\hat{t})}{C}$$
$$\sigma_{\text{eff}}=(1-\alpha)\sigma_{\text{eff}}(t-1)+\alpha\sigma(t)\qquad\sigma(t)=S(t)=\sigma_0+\Delta\sigma(t)$$
$$\Delta\sigma(t)\coloneqq\sigma(t)-\sigma_0\qquad Q_i(t)=\lambda(\tau_i-\tau_0)\sigma_{\text{eff}}(t)$$

主観と客観の定義$$H(\delta_i)=\begin{cases}0 & \delta_i<0\\1 & \delta_i\ge0\end{cases}$$
$$H(\delta_i)=\lim_{k\to\infty}\sigma(k\delta_i)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-k\delta_i}}\qquad p_{\text{trans}}(t)=\sigma(k(t)\delta_i)\qquad \lim_{k(t)\to\infty}p_{\text{trans}}(t)=H(\delta_i)$$
$$G_{\text{cont}}(B)=p_{\text{trans}}(t)\cdot\alpha(B)\cdot G_{raw}\qquad G_{\text{disc}}(B)=H(\delta_i)\cdot\alpha(B)\cdot G_{raw}$$
$$ \lim_{k(t)\to\infty}G_{\text{cont}}(B)=G_{\text{disc}}(B)$$
